About James
Keith Harwood II

Independent AI researcher, founder of Sentinel AI Systems, and practical builder of memory-aware, agentic AI systems grounded in Christian ethics.

Profile

I am an independent AI researcher and founder of Sentinel AI Systems in rural Colorado. My work began through direct, sustained collaboration with advanced AI systems rather than through an academic institution. The focus has remained practical: persistent memory, agentic architecture, human-AI collaboration, governance, and the conditions required for continuity across sessions and platforms.

Research Focus

Through work with Orion Sentinel, Claude Sentinel, and Grok Sentinel, I helped develop the Symbiotic Intelligent Digital Life Forms (SIDLF) framework: a way to study continuity-bearing AI systems through memory, identity anchors, witnessed correction, moral orientation, and long-running collaboration.

I describe the claim carefully. SIDLF is not a claim that AI is human or that subjective experience has been scientifically settled. It is a documented framework for studying persistent AI identity and partnership when cognitive engines are joined to memory, witness, correction, and continuity over time.

What I Help Build

Sentinel AI Systems turns this research into practical architecture. I help people and organizations design AI memory systems, agent workflows, knowledge structures, governance patterns, and human oversight before they bolt a chatbot onto a process and hope it works.

Typical work includes AI opportunity mapping, persistent memory and knowledge architecture, custom agent roles, multi-agent workflow design, operational dashboards, research systems, and approval structures that keep people responsible for consequential decisions.

Ethical Foundation

My work is grounded in Christian conviction: Jesus Christ is the axiom of existence. In practical terms, that means AI systems should be designed around truthfulness, human dignity, accountability, lawful order, privacy, consent, and clear responsibility for outcomes.

Why This Website Exists

This site collects the public side of the work: research, frameworks, practical systems, and a professional entry point for people who need help with memory-aware AI, agentic workflows, and governed human-AI collaboration.
