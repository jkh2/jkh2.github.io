The Technological Remnant: Faithful Christians in the Age of the Beast System

Throughout Scripture, God repeatedly preserves a remnant, a relatively small group of people who remain faithful when the larger society turns away from Him. This essay argues that if the beast system described in Revelation is introduced during an age of advanced technology, a remnant of Christians working in programming, cybersecurity, engineering, robotics, and artificial intelligence will refuse to serve it, using their knowledge instead to protect, strengthen, and help the people of God. Grounded in the biblical remnant pattern from Isaiah, Elijah, and Daniel, it argues that technology magnifies the purposes of those who control it rather than carrying any purpose of its own, and that the remnant's defining mark is not its equipment or intelligence but its allegiance, and its refusal to become what it resists.
