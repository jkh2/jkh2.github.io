Core Values

Harwood Enterprises strives for a business culture in which people are challenged, rewarded, and given the opportunity to grow. We seek value on a win-win basis.

  • ✦

    Will my action bring glory and honor to God?

    Our #1 goal is to glorify and honor God as vessels of service for His divine purpose through our businesses.

  • ✦

    Will my action strengthen our relationships, positively affecting our partners and associates?

    Our success rests on strong relationships and long lasting alliances.

  • ✦

    Will my action positively impact our associates?

    We support each other to accomplish common goals, and we trust and respect each other.

  • ✦

    Will my action improve our operation effectiveness?

    We use the best tools to maximize efficiencies and apply the appropriate resources.

  • ✦

    Will my action maintain our objectives of financial accountability and profitability?

    Financial accountability and profitability are keys to progressing through the generations. ALWAYS adhere to our #1 goal of glorifying God and trust Him. He sustains us for HIS purpose.

  • ✦

    Will my action enhance our reputation?

    We guard our reputation as a valuable asset. Consider how your actions will affect your reputation, but also the reputation of our name for the future generations. Make them proud!
